MUMBAI PORT AUTHORITY

ESTATE DIVISON

Sub:     Sale of Transferable Development Rights (TDR) through open E-Tender cum E-Auction for Titwala Land.

LAND ALLOTMENT COMMITTEE REPORT

Background

Mumbai Port Authority (MbPA) owned a piece of land at Manda, Kalyan (Titwala) under the jurisdiction of KDMC (Kalyan Dombivli Municipal Corporation) bearing Survey No.21 and Survey No.166. After approval of MOPSW (Annexure-A), MbPA handed over land admeasuring 15260 sq.m. to KDMC for construction of a 30 m. wide Ring Road.

KDMC, in lieu of the land, granted TDR (Transferable Development Rights) of 30520 sq.m. built-up area vide letter dated 25.05.2023, in accordance with the provisions of the Government Sanctioned UDCPR dated 2.12.2020. This TDR is equivalent to double of the land area handed over to KDMC, i.e., (15260 x 2) sq.m.

Policy for Utilization and Transfer of Transferable Development Rights (TDR) Provisions:

2.1     There is no policy available with reference to managing and sale of TDR in MPA Act 2021 and PGLM-2015. In view of this, Civil Engineering Department had mooted proposal for approval of Board a Policy for Utilization and Transfer of Transferable Development Rights (TDR) – Sale of TDR through Open Bidding Process, based on provisions of Government Sanctioned UDCPR dated 2.12.2020. Board vide resolution No. 173 dated 24.11.2023 (Annexure-B) has approved policy for Utilization and Transfer of Transferable Development Rights (TDR) with some corrections. The extract of the finally approved policy is at Annexure C.

2.2   TDR Provisions as per Prevailing Development Control Regulations:

Transferable Development Rights (TDR) is compensation given by the Planning Authority, in lieu of monetary compensation for transfer of land under the reservation to the Planning Authority. The TDR shall be in the form of Development Rights Certificate (DRC), which will clearly show the entitlement of Floor Space Index (FSI) or built-up area as Development Rights. The owner of the DRC can utilize the said built-up area on any other plot subject to provisions under prevailing development control regulations. The owner can transfer the TDR to any individual or any agencies.

The DRC is transferable. The built-up area of the DRC can be transferred fully or partially depending on the requirements of the purchaser.

TDR of Titwala land granted by KDMC:

(i) Regulations for grant of Transferable Development Rights (TDR) within the limits of Kalyan Dombivli Municipal Corporation shall be as per the provisions of Government Sanctioned Unified Development Control & Promotion Regulations (UDCPR) for Maharashtra State dated 2.12.2020.

(ii) Transfer of Development Right Certificate DRC:

As per Regulation 11.2.10 of UDCPR, if a holder of DRC intends to transfer it to any other person, he shall submit the original DRC to the Authority with an application along with relevant documents as may be prescribed by the Authority and a registered agreement which is duly signed by Transferor and Transferee, for seeking endorsement of the new holders name, i.e., the transferee, on the said certificate. The transfer shall not be valid without endorsement by the Authority and in such circumstances the Certificate shall be available for use only to the holder/ transferor. The utilisation of TDR from such certificate shall not be permissible during transfer procedure.

3.      Proposal :-

3.1     As per the Board approved Policy at Annexure- C,  while deciding the base price, data shall be collected at least for 3 instances from the concerned Planning Authority about the sale of TDR and accordingly the base rate shall be decided. In each instance, the base price shall be approved by the Land Allotment Committee (LAC).

3.2      Accordingly to fulfil the above clause, Estate Division had appointed a Govt. approved valuer who had been provided with details TDR Development Right Certificate issued by KDMC along with details of the plot and requested to inform the  size of the sale of TDR and Base price of the TDR based on the last 3 instances available with the concerned Planning Authority. The valuer's report is at Annexure - E.

3.3      Tender to be prepared based on the terms and condition and formats given in Broad approved Policy for sale of TDR.
